# Agenda

The course runs across **three days, three hours each**, for nine hours of content in total. Each day has a **15-minute break after the first 90 minutes**. There are no fixed clock times, so schedule the start to suit your timezone and audience.

## Day 1

| Segment | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Welcome and objectives | 5 min |
| **Intro to the UiPath Platform** | 15 min |
| **1 · Studio, Orchestrator & Licensing** | 35 min |
| **2 · DataTables & Excel** | 35 min |
| Break | 15 min |
| **3 · Reliable UI Automation in SAP S/4HANA** | 75 min |

## Day 2

| Segment | Duration |

| Segment | Duration |

| Break | 15 min |

## The through-line

Each module builds on the last. By Module 5 you are combining skills, reading an email attachment, extracting PDF text with string methods, and recording the result. By Module 6 you are structuring all of it into a project you could hand to a teammate.

**Shape data, automate reliably, handle failure, structure professionally.**

Day 3 then applies these foundations to the desktop **SAP GUI** and closes with SAP and classic implementation best practices.
